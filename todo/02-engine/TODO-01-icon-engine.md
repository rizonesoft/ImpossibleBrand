# TODO-01 -- Icon Engine

> **Goal:** The icon engine lives here: it renders, generates from shared parts, validates, drafts, scores, polishes, promotes and rejects icons, consumers run it from their pinned commit to draft and sync, and a gallery shows the whole set.

> **Current state:** Measured 2026-11-25. Nothing of the engine exists anywhere: Impossible OS planned it as `todo/08-graphics-ui/TODO-01-graphics-asset-foundation.md` §8 to §10, all unbuilt, and those sections move here (they close in the OS behind their import). The renders were made by the OS's `scripts/convert-icons.sh` (rsvg-convert at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 72, 96, 128 and 256 into the OS tree only). Two sources are generated by OS scripts: `icons/src/recycle_bin_full.svg` by `scripts/site/gen_recycle_bin_full.py` (113 lines) and the silk wallpapers by `scripts/site/gen_wallpapers.py` (198 lines), both at OS commit `75c468c32`. `rsvg-convert` and ImageMagick are installed in WSL.

## Inputs

- `docs/specs/2026-11-25-brand-split-design.md`: "What happens when something needs an icon that does not exist?" (draft, request, collect, replace) and the phase 3 row.
- `docs/icons.md`: the art rules every validation and score point enforces.
- Impossible OS at `75c468c32`: `todo/08-graphics-ui/TODO-01-graphics-asset-foundation.md` §8 (three modes, parts, determinism, fingerprints, direct-edit protection), §9 (draft mode, `draft` and `part new` commands, tile-plus-glyph floor) and §10 (rubric, `score`, `sheet`, 5-round polish, promotion, `reject`, gallery): the behaviour copied here, cited per item.
- D01 T01: `palette.json`, `tools/palette.py resolve`, `contract/os.json`, `icons/status.json`, `tools/pack.py` and `tools/check.py`.

## Outcome

- `python3 tools/icon_engine.py build` regenerates exactly the icons whose inputs changed, renders them into `icons/color/` and `SOURCES.sha256`, and a second run changes nothing.
- A consumer runs `icon_engine.py draft` and `sync` from its pinned commit, and an icon missing from the pack becomes a local draft and a request without any person.

**Adjacency:** list=applicable @ D02 T01 §16; document=not-applicable (the engine produces art files, not documents); settings=not-applicable (behaviour is fixed by the rules in docs/icons.md and the contract, not tunables); reporting=applicable @ D02 T01 §10; notifications=not-applicable (requests are committed files, not notices); permissions=not-applicable (public repository, no roles); audit=applicable @ D02 T01 §13; exchange=applicable @ D02 T01 §15; reverse=applicable @ D02 T01 §14

**Adjacency rationale:** The gallery is how an asset is found without knowing its name (§16). The score file is the report (§10). Promotion records the score and round count beside each icon, the audit trail (§13). Sync is the exchange with consumers (§15). Rejection is the undo of a promotion (§14).
